Treadmill Day Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Introduction

Treadmill Day is an iPhone app that reads the distance and time from a photo of your treadmill display and keeps a log of your runs.

The short version: we do not collect your data. Your runs stay on your device, and in your own iCloud if you choose to sync.

What the app stores

  • Your runs: the date, miles, time, and (if you add one by photo) a small copy of the photo of the treadmill display.
  • Your settings, such as whether iCloud sync is on.

All of this is saved on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Photos and the camera

The app asks for camera access only so you can photograph your treadmill display. You can also pick a photo from your library; the app sees only the photo you choose, not the rest of your library.

The numbers are read from the photo by Apple's text recognition, which runs on your device. The photo is not uploaded to us or to any third party for this. You can also add a run by typing it in, without any photo.

iCloud

The app has an optional "Sync with iCloud" setting, on by default. When it is on, your runs and their photos are stored in your own private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, so that your history appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. We cannot access this data. You can turn sync off in the Settings tab; the change applies the next time you open the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, see, or have access to your runs or photos.
  • We do not require an account or sign-up.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

If you choose to share app analytics with developers in your iPhone's privacy settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage statistics. These do not identify you.

Exporting your data

You can export your whole history as a CSV file (date, miles, time, and pace) from the History tab. The file is created on your device. You choose where it goes, such as Files, AirDrop, or email, and we do not receive it.

Deleting your data

You can delete a run by swiping it left in the History tab. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.

If iCloud sync is on, deleting a run on one device also removes it from your other devices, and the synced copy stays in your iCloud until you remove it. You can manage it under Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Account Storage. If you reinstall the app with sync on, your synced runs come back.

Children

Treadmill Day is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Health information

Treadmill Day is a personal running log. It does not give medical advice and does not connect to Apple Health.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page with a new effective date.
